# Video brief

FORMAT:   Long (search how-to / build it / workflow / A vs B / tested it / fix / news hands-on)
          or Short (tip / fail and fix / A vs B / numbered list / before and after / news demo).
PACKAGE:  The chosen title and thumbnail from package.md. The video delivers exactly what they promise, early.
AUDIENCE: Who this is for and what they already know (from channel.md).
OUTCOME:  The one thing they can do after watching.
HOOK:     What is on screen and said in the first 5 seconds (first second for a Short). Show the result, not a promise of it.
EVIDENCE: Use only src/data/<video>.json, the screen recordings and the files I list. Never hardcode figures.
NEVER:    Never invent a number. If a fact is missing, stop and tell me.
          No intro, logo or greeting before the hook. No subscribe ask before the payoff. Method gets one line.
          Plus the never list in channel.md.
